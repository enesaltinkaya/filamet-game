- Before hijacking a "spare" `ShadowMapAttribs` field for a new knob, grep the RECEIVERS for it — the receivers, not DiligentFX, decide whether a slot is free. `fVSMLightBleedingReduction` looks unused (no FX sampler is called on this path) but `splat_terrain_ps.hlsl` reads it as `sVSMParams.y` in `chebyshevUpperBound`/`filterShadowVSM`, and sending `4.0` there makes `saturate((p-4)/(1-4))` = 1 for every p, i.e. the moments receivers report lit everywhere. Fingerprint: the shadow is missing in VSM/EVSM at a vantage where `ENGINE_SHADOW_MODE=1` (PCF, which never reads the field) draws it fine, and the moments tap path is otherwise healthy (`ENGINE_SHADOW_ATLAS_DUMP` shows a correct caster silhouette). The carrier that works is `iMaxAnisotropy`: FX's anisotropic PCF sampler (its stock job) is never called here, and the engine default value is already 4, so the field's value does not change at all. Mirror an int slot with its real type (`int sFilterMaxTexels` after a `float3`, one 16-byte group) — a `float4` mirror reads the int bits as a denormal.
- A world-space shadow filter footprint without an ATLAS-TEXEL cap turns a small caster into a blob: `ENGINE_SHADOW_FILTER_WORLD` 0.12 m is 20 texels wide in the 6 m ring cascade and 1.5 texels in the 80 m one, so the cascade that covers the player blurs her silhouette by 20 texels and limbs merge. Both halves must be capped together — `ShadowMapManager::SetMaxFilterRadiusTexels` (the `ConvertToFilterable` pre-blur) and the receiver's Poisson tap radius (`tapScale = clamp(min(fFilterWorldSize/texelWorld, cap), 0.1, cap)`, never `min(fFilterWorldSize, cap/texelWorld)`: mixing metres with texels yields tapScale = 0.12 and a shadow that fades to nothing at that vantage). Capping at 4 texels: parked player-shadow crop gradient 5.58 → 5.86, dark px 20642 → 15620, head/arm/leg silhouette restored; coarse cascades already sit under the cap, so the cascade-boundary penumbra match is untouched.
- Multiplying every covering cascade (the union composition) fattens a near caster's shadow: the fine cascade's sharp attenuation gets multiplied by the coarse cascade's 20-texel-blurred value, so the penumbra width is the COARSE cascade's, not the one that resolves the caster. Lit-wins (finest covering cascade authoritative, the props receiver's policy) is now the default with the player-anchored rings: full-frame diff at the parked vantage 35832 px brighter vs 1941 darker, concentrated in the player's penumbra ring with the only other changes 1-px lines along cascade box edges — no broad light leak where a coarse cascade used to darken, consecutive-frame attenuation drift unchanged (0.078 → 0.082 gray). `ENGINE_SHADOW_UNION=1` restores the multiply for A/B.
