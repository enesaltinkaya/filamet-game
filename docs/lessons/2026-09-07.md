## 2026-09-07 — Camera-anchored casters: a shadow caster whose placement is prebuilt in anchor space must be rebuilt with THIS frame's anchor before the shadow pass — otherwise the caster is displaced by dEye (one frame of camera-eye travel) and the player's ground shadow detaches while the orbit camera moves

**Rule:** every caster that renders "positions minus the anchor" must see the SAME anchor as the light matrices and the receivers, in the same frame. The glTF player's pose is rebuilt in `worldDraw` → `poseRebuild()` (placement root from `diligentWorldAnchor()`), but the shadow pass runs FIRST in `draw()` — so `gltfDiligentShadowDraw` renders `transforms` pinned to LAST frame's anchor. The depth silhouette lands exactly `dEye = eyeCurr − eyePrev` away from the player; the receiver samples it with the CURRENT light matrix, so the ground shadow is displaced by one frame of eye travel — which during an orbit drag is metres per second (eye speed = orbitRadius × yawRate). An animation-only pose lag is pixels; the anchor lag is the visible bug. Fix: call `poseRebuild()` at the top of `gltfDiligentShadowDraw` (idempotent within a frame — animation advances only in the update phase). Terrain and props casters subtract the CURRENT anchor in their VS (`cbShadowPass.f4AnchorHi/Lo`, frame attribs `f4ExtraData[3..4]`), which is why only the player's shadow lagged.

**Fingerprint:** artifact appears ONLY while the orbit camera moves (any TAA weight), displaced along the orbit's tangent, magnitude ≈ per-frame eye travel (small at slow drag, ~1 m at a fast flick); converges to correct over ~stability/(1−stability) frames (0.9 → ~0.5 s at 60 fps) after the drag stops — TAA's history holding the displaced shading makes the lag look exponential. TAA OFF kills the convergence tail but leaves the (smaller, exactly-one-frame) displacement; verified MVs are correct (signed `ENGINE_MV_DUMP` vs CPU-reprojected flow within jitter tolerance) — the fault is upstream in the shadow map content, not the reprojection.

**Incident:** "when I rotate the camera, player shadow can't keep up; it converges to the correct location half a second later." Reproduced with `ENGINE_FAKE_DRAG=25` + `ENGINE_AUTO_RUN=1` (`ENGINE_SCREENSHOT` disables player mode — set `ENGINE_AUTO_RUN` to get it back): pre-fix TAA-on capture shows the shadow head detached from the feet, TAA-off and post-fix captures attach. The 2026-09-07 `gltfDiligentShadowDraw` entry already flagged "pose lags one frame" — the missed consequence was that the placement ANCHOR rides in that same pose, so the lag scales with camera rotation speed, not just with animation motion.

## 2026-09-07 — RenderDoc capture run: `diligent: Required extension VK_KHR_wayland_surface is not available` → segfault at `CreateDeviceAndContextsVk`, only with the capture env var — the implicit layer filters the extension away, and the local Diligent build requires it unconditionally

**Rule:** a Vulkan implicit layer (RenderDoc's) re-filters `vkEnumerateInstanceExtensionProperties` to its own static support table, so enabling the layer can make extensions "disappear" from a working config. The local RenderDoc build has no Wayland support (`ENABLE_UNSUPPORTED_EXPERIMENTAL_POSSIBLY_BROKEN_WAYLAND=OFF` in its CMakeCache), so with `ENABLE_VULKAN_RENDERDOC_CAPTURE=1` active, `VK_KHR_wayland_surface` is filtered out — while Diligent's Linux build hardcodes `VK_USE_PLATFORM_WAYLAND_KHR=1` (`DiligentCore/BuildTools/CMake/VulkanUtils.cmake`), making `CreateDeviceAndContextsVk` throw on the missing "required" extension. Fingerprint: identical binary boots clean without the capture env (layer inactive), segfaults at device creation with it; the extension list printed right before the throw is missing `VK_KHR_wayland_surface` but still contains `VK_EXT_xcb_surface`/`VK_KHR_xlib_surface`/`VK_KHR_surface`. On a Wayland session the windowing side is a second, independent gate: the engine's Vk surface path is X11-only (`DiligentRenderer.cpp` reads only `SDL_PROP_WINDOW_X11_*`), so the run must force `SDL_VIDEO_BACKEND=x11` (XWayland) to get a usable window handle at all.

**Incident:** first test capture on the new engine segfaulted on a Plasma/Wayland session; fixed by removing `VK_USE_PLATFORM_WAYLAND_KHR` from the local Diligent checkout (rebuild `cpp-thirdparty/diligent/git/build-linux`) and exporting `SDL_VIDEO_BACKEND=x11` in `scripts/run.sh`'s renderdoc branch. Capture verified: `renderdoccmd thumb` on the .rdc renders the frame.

## 2026-09-07 — DiligentFX shadow port: ShadowAttribs.fFixedDepthBias is NOT in cascade NDC depth units — normalize it to the cascade's light-space z scale or the PCF bias swallows the whole depth band and every receiver renders lit

**Rule:** when porting DiligentFX shadows, `ShadowMapAttribs.fFixedDepthBias` is expected to be ~1e-4–1e-3 OF THE CASCADE'S DEPTH RANGE, an absolute NDC/z-lerp value. It feeds `FilterShadowCascade`'s `FractionalSamplingError` which is SUBTRACTED from the receiver depth before the LESS comparison — so a value set in raw units (e.g. a hand-copied 0.005) against a cascade whose `f4LightSpaceScale.z` is ~0.1 becomes ~30 texels of depth and pushes every PCF tap to pass: **all-lit shadows with a perfectly healthy depth pass**. Fix at the ShadowAttribs fill site (here `ShadowDiligent.cpp updateFrameImpl`): set it AFTER `DistributeCascades` and scale by `Cascades[0].f4LightSpaceScale.z` (0.005 × 0.1069 ≈ 5.3e-4 ≈ 2.5 texels). Fingerprint: uniform light amount 1.0 on every receiver while cascade-0 readback shows geometry (min > 0, bbox (0,0)-(1,1)), no VUIDs, cascade selection correct. Diagnostic ladder that found it, in order — reuse it: `ENGINE_SHADOW_DEBUG_VIS=8` (cbuffer fields as colours — ruled out the +32 B `fCascadeCamSpaceZEnd` float[8]/float4[2] C++/HLSL layout asymmetry hypothesis), `=9/10` (hw SampleCmp vs manual Load, real-path UV vs depth-pass UV — ruled out sampler/SRV/UV; note a hardcoded-cascade probe disagrees out-of-cascade because Load OOB returns 0 while clamp+border 1.0 makes SampleCmp lit — an OOB/clamp artifact, not a broken comparison), `=2/1` (cascade index / light amount — failure is INSIDE FilterShadowCascade), `=11` (FractionalSamplingError as colour — measured 0.0061 ≈ 30 texels).

**Incident:** "shadows are not working" on the Diligent path (cascaded D32 atlas + DiligentFX PCF sampling in terrain/props lit passes). Seven rounds of probes narrowed an all-lit failure to the depth bias: fFixedDepthBias = 0.005 was 82% of a measured FractionalSamplingError of 0.0061 (~30× the per-texel depth delta of ~2.1e-4). Normalizing to the cascade z scale fixed it — visible shadows, healthy readback, no new VUIDs.

## 2026-09-07 — pak_1.pak shader-repack: the game loads runtime-compiled HLSL from the packed pak, NOT the loose `c-game/data/pak_1/materials/` tree — a shader edit does nothing until the repack runs, and the md5-stamp cache can silently skip

**Rule:** before believing any negative result from a shader edit, check what the game actually reads: `unzip -p build/c-game/data/pak_1.pak materials/<name>.hlsl | grep <your-marker>`. The build copies `c-engine/.../shaders/*.hlsl` into `c-game/data/pak_1/materials/` then zips into `build/c-game/data/pak_1.pak`; both the copy and the zip have cache stamps that can skip a stale-looking rebuild. Keep the loose sources and the pak copies identical (compare md5) when debugging sampling math; keep `./scripts/build.sh` in the loop after every .hlsl touch.

**Incident:** shadow-sampling probes 9/10/11 showed stale behaviour until the repack was traced; also `heightmap_terrain_ps.hlsl` `PSTerrainIn` was missing the `float4 PosInLightProjSpace : LIGHT_PROJ_POS;` field (a latent compile bug in the probe path, fixed in the loose source then repacked).

## 2026-09-07 — Shadow-map readback: transition the depth texture from RESOURCE_STATE_DEPTH_WRITE (its actual tracked state right after the shadow pass), not DEPTH_READ — otherwise every readback frame fires VUID-VkImageMemoryBarrier-oldLayout-01197

**Rule:** when inserting a mid-frame CopyTexture readback of a texture the engine just used as a depth attachment, the tracked Diligent state is DEPTH_WRITE — transitioning it from a hardcoded DEPTH_READ makes Vulkan see a barrier whose oldLayout doesn't match the current layout: `VUID-VkImageMemoryBarrier-oldLayout-01197` on image 'Shadow map SRV' every readback frame. Use the state the pass actually left the resource in (both the to-COPY_SOURCE and the back transition, `ShadowDiligent.cpp` ENGINE_SHADOW_READBACK block). The informational "Texture 'Shadow map SRV' is currently bound as depth buffer…" line is expected and harmless.

**Incident:** baseline VUID-01197 on 'Shadow map SRV' reproduced in every run since round 1; readback transitions switched DEPTH_READ → DEPTH_WRITE, VUID gone, readback data unchanged (cascade bbox healthy).

## 2026-09-07 — Drawing a `GLTF_PBR_Renderer` model into the CSM depth atlas: a `MAP_WRITE` without `MAP_FLAG_DISCARD` does not advance the dynamic-buffer ring, and DiligentFX PBR shaders consume UNtransposed matrices — the game's glslang shaders the transposed one

**Rule:** two independent traps when re-rendering the PBR character from the shadow pass with the light view-proj swapped into `g_Frame.Camera.mViewProj`:
1. `frameAttribsCB` is a dynamic buffer: mapping `MAP_WRITE` with `MAP_FLAG_NONE` returns the ring region the LAST `MAP_FLAG_DISCARD` map landed on, so the draw reads the stale world camera and renders with the wrong matrix entirely — the write silently lands one region behind. Always `MAP_FLAG_DISCARD` (the lessons.md dynamic-buffer rule, now with its shadow-pass fingerprint).
2. Matrix packing is per-shader-set: DiligentFX PBR shaders compile with `SHADER_COMPILE_FLAG_PACK_MATRIX_ROW_MAJOR` and consume the RAW matrix (that's how `fillFrameAttribs` uploads `view * proj`), while the game's runtime-glslang depth shaders need `Transpose()`. Feeding the PBR VS the transposed light matrix gives perspective-like clip.w values from an orthographic projection and full clipping — zero depth written, no error anywhere.

**Fingerprint:** shadow-pass player draw present (right VBO, cascade DSV bound, depth LESS + writes, all verts posted) but the atlas A/B (`ENGINE_SHADOW_NO_PLAYER=1` vs unset) diffs at wind-noise level. RenderDoc `GetPostVSData` on the player draw is the decider: `w ≈ 4.3..5.2` = stale world camera (trap 1); `w = -0.3..-0.1` from an ortho light proj whose clip.w must be exactly 1 = transposed matrix (trap 2). Dumping the bound cbuffer bytes at the draw proves WHICH data the draw sees before doubting the upload path. Also: binding the full-res world color RTs beside the 2048 DSV trips `VUID-VkRenderingInfo-pNext-06079` (render area > depth attachment) — use throwaway cascade-sized dummy RTVs sized from the DSV texture.

**Incident:** "i cant see player character's shadows" — the CSM pass only drew terrain + props; the player was never a caster. Added `gltfDiligentShadowDraw` (re-renders the character through `GLTF_PBR_Renderer` per cascade with the light view-proj in the PBR frame attribs; pose lags one frame because the shadow pass runs before `poseRebuild`). Both traps above hit in sequence; after the row-major fix the atlas A/B shows a coherent character blob and the world shows the humanoid shadow.

## 2026-09-07 — Behind-the-camera casters are clipped by the near cascades' light cubes: with DiligentFX `StabilizeExtents` the cascade light volume is the frustum's minimum bounding sphere as a light-space CUBE, which does not reach behind the camera — the only in-engine lever is the caster FOV pad (`casterPad` on the projection handed to `DistributeCascades`), default raised 1.35 → 2.8

**Rule:** a caster at negative camera-space z (behind the eye) sits outside every cascade's light cube unless the cube's back reach along camera-forward exceeds the caster's distance behind the camera. With `StabilizeExtents=true` the cube is `GetFrustumMinimumBoundingSphere` → light-space AABB: center at the sphere center (≈ (0,0,Far) in camera space), half-extent R = Far·√k2, k2 = 1/(p·cot(h/2))² + 1/(p·cot(v/2))² for pad p. Back reach along camera-forward ≈ Far·(0.919 + √k2) for this scene's oblique sun (light axes vs camera: fwd ≈ 0.919·lx − 0.394·lz) — the oblique sun is what lets the cube reach behind at all, and the reach grows with p (wider FOV → bigger sphere). `AdjustCascadeRange` can't help: minZ is clamped to `kCameraNear` and the library `VERIFY(fCascadeNearZ > 0)` — negative near is not available without a third-party change. Raising the pad is write/read consistent automatically (depth pass, player caster and PS sampling all consume the same `DistributeCascades` output).

**Fingerprint:** a caster just out of view behind the camera casts a shadow on a receiver near the camera that is a set of nested STRAIGHT-EDGED polygons (projected light-cube faces), one hard tonal step per cascade zEnd split, and the nearest band (0..zEnd0, 0–2.6 m) fully LIT while the tree's shadow should cover it (caster clipped out of cascade-0's cube). `ENGINE_SHADOW_READBACK=N ENGINE_SHADOW_DUMP=path` (depth dumps as P5) shows the cube's clipped hexagon in the atlas.

**Incident:** "player standing in a tree's shadow, tree behind the camera — the shadow looks like two cut-off cascade levels." A/B via `ENGINE_SHADOW_CASTER_PAD` (1.35 default → 2.4/2.8/3.5/4.0) with a shadow-mask IoU check against a single-cascade ground truth (shadowQuality 0, 1 cascade 40 m): base 0.537, pad 2.6 0.981, pad 2.8+ 0.984 (saturation — 2.8/3.5/4.0 identical) → baked 2.8 in `ShadowDiligent.cpp`. Cost: light-cube area ×(2.8/1.35)² ≈ 4.3, i.e. effective shadow texel density ~2× coarser everywhere — revisit with a targeted backward caster extension (true caster frustum) if near-shadow sharpness becomes a problem.

## 2026-09-07 — A CSM quality tier's "shadow distance" is only the cascade near/far split, not the coverage: with `StabilizeExtents` the cascade light cube (bounding sphere of the caster-padded frustum) reaches ~3× the tier distance, so shadows sample far beyond it — enforce the tier distance with a receiver-side fade, and never shrink the cube to fix far shadows (re-introduces the caster-clip cutout)

**Rule:** in DiligentFX `DistributeCascades`, `AdjustCascadeRange(maxZ = tier.distanceM)` only bounds the view-z partition the cascade frustum is built from. The actual shadowed volume is the cascade's light-space cube — `GetFrustumMinimumBoundingSphere` of the (2.8× caster-padded) frustum, radius ≈ Far·√(tan²(h/2)p² + tan²(v/2)p²) ≈ 66 m at the lowest tier (1024/1-cascade/20 m), i.e. ~132 m across. `BEST_CASCADE_SEARCH` receiver sampling rejects only by the cube's margin box, so receivers/casters well past the tier distance still get shadowed. The fix is a RECEIVER fade, not a smaller cube: the terrain/props PS lerp `fLightAmount → 1` (lit) over the last 25 % of the tier distance (`f4ShadowFade` tail of the terrain/props frame cbuffers, filled from `shadowDiligentTierDistance()`; `ENGINE_SHADOW_FADE=0` A/B-knob). Shrinking the cube (lowering the pad) to shorten the reach instead re-introduces the caster-clip cutout: casters (overhead canopy) outside the small cube stop writing depth, and the shadow terminates at a projected cube face — hard straight polygon edges on the ground (see the 2026-09-07 caster-pad entry; the pad stays 2.8).

**Fingerprint:** tier distance 20 m but tree shadows visible out to ~60–80 m at the lowest quality — the one-shot `shadow dbg: cascade 0 scale` log line gives the cube size directly (extent = 2/scale ≈ 132 m at pad 2.8, ≈ 47 m at pad 1). Distinguishing the two "hard polygon on the ground" artifacts: A/B `shadowMode=0` — the blob vanishing means it is CSM; with the pad 2.8 cube a persisting hexagonal blob is the CANOPY's own shadow silhouette (natural, antialiased), while straight edges that shrink/move with the pad are projected light-cube faces (caster clip). `ENGINE_SHADOW_CASTER_PAD=1` A/B shows the cube face crossing the shadowed ground inside the fade range — the receiver fade cannot hide a missing-caster edge, only a too-far one.

**Incident:** "at lowest shadow quality the distance is supposed to be 20 m, but I see tree shadows farther" + "caster pad = 1 looks better, but then I get the cutout." Added the receiver fade (both world PSs + cbuffer tail + `shadowDiligentTierDistance()`); kept pad 2.8. Verified headless (`ENGINE_SCREENSHOT` + `ENGINE_SCREENSHOT_FRAME=900`, shadowQuality 0): far-ground A/B (`ENGINE_SHADOW_FADE=0` vs default) — distant tree shadows gone at default, present at 0; the canopy-shadow blob and its hexagonal silhouette are unchanged (it is the canopy, not the cube).
