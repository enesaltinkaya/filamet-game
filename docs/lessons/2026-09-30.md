- Player-anchored cascade rings apply to every shadow mode, not only PCF: `ShadowMapManager::ConvertToFilterable` consumes `ShadowAttribs` after the engine's ring override, so EVSM gets the same player-relative cascade geometry. Rings off (`ENGINE_SHADOW_RINGS=0`) restores camera-frustum distribution for A/B.
- "Small triangle-shaped shadow, top-right of screen, over terrain, gone with shadows off" = cascade NEAR-plane cross-section: ring cascade z windows are `plz ± 2·radius` (light-z centred on the player), so terrain/props between the sun and the cascade get clipped at the near plane and write depth ≈0 across their projected area — a planar occluder that covers every receiver behind it, with straight box-face edges. Measured signature in the atlas: the map corner holds depth 0.008 (cascade 1, prop write) / 0.298 (cascade 0, cliff write) at the corner texel, and per-draw bisect attributes it to the first caster of that slice. Fix in the ring block: `nearPad = 2·radius·ENGINE_SHADOW_NEAR_PAD` (default 3) dilates the window to `plz − 2r − nearPad … plz + 2r`, putting every caster that can cast into the cascade (cliff base 25 m, cliff wall 850 m, mountains 700 m here) inside the window as a real occluder; the cull follows the caster matrix for free. Cost is depth-only: cascade-0 z quantization 1.2 → 2.3 cm/texel (window 24 → 48 m); PCF slope bias scales with the window (margin 12 → 24 cm), EVSM variance floor scales in world units. A VSM-mode Chebyshev floor of 1e-4 in normalized window units bleeds ≈1 % of window (16 m at cascade 2): VSM needs the floor rescaled per window if mode 2 is ever the shipping mode.
- A cascade box "anchored to the player" is not anchored if its snap coordinate is (player − camera anchor): the texel grid rides on the camera, so camera motion re-picks the centre in texel steps every frame and the ENTIRE atlas re-lays. The ring block's `clx = round((playerLight − anchorLight)/texel)` did exactly that. Fix: snap in absolute light space (grid origin = world) and derive the bias as `−(centreAbs − anchorLight)·scale`; the box centre is then constant while the player is parked, and the bias keeps the caster/receiver matrices anchor-relative (no f32 cancellation). Fingerprint: `ENGINE_SHADOW_ATLAS_DUMP` per-frame readback — 100 % of cascade-0/1 depth texels change between consecutive dolly frames (max Δdepth 0.126 NDC), 0 % with the world grid; a static-camera run is 0 % either way. Dolly shadow-edge residual (parallax-compensated attenuation map, 0.035 m/frame) 38.2 → 2.90 gray, cascade-0 core 38.5 → 1.46; `ENGINE_SHADOW_RING_GRID=0` restores the old grid for A/B. Incident: "shimmering on the edges of shadows when the camera is moving".
- After the grid fix, what is left of the dolly edge shimmer is the cascade BLEND band (the only camera-depth-driven term: `dist = (zend − viewZ)/(zend − zstart)`), not the raw render: residual 13.65 gray at filter 0.04/transition 0.1 → 9.74 with `ENGINE_SHADOW_FILTER_WORLD` 0.12 → 8.52 at `ENGINE_SHADOW_TRANSITION` 0.3 → 5.58 at 0.5 (band 4 m wide).
