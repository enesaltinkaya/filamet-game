# 2026-09-30

- A jitter-subtracted terrain motion vector mis-aligns the TAA history by exactly the jitter delta: static PCF temporal variance measured 59.98 gray over a 25-frame burst. `splat_terrain_ps.hlsl` now emits total apparent motion (`ndcCurr - ndcPrev`, the PBR pass' convention); with `TAA_MOTION_VECTOR_DIFF_FACTOR=16` the same static measurement is 2.12 gray. Fingerprint: dolly/static edge shimmer unchanged by PCF tuning but collapses when the reprojection offset is fixed.
- The library's 256 history-motion factor kills TAA at sub-pixel jitter motion; 16 keeps a usable history at dolly speeds. Deterministic fixed-dt pairs: PCF 22.92 -> 21.42 gray, EVSM2 19.93 -> 19.91. Lowering the factor was previously measured as ineffective only while terrain motion vectors excluded jitter.
- PCF 3x3 hard comparison sampling makes the blue cube's broad shadow edge re-quantize every frame under camera motion. EVSM2's filterable moments pass measures 19.91 vs 22.92 gray (`shadowstd` over the shadow mask, p95 38.01 vs 41.33) and measured 2.88 ms GPU vs 3.24 ms for PCF at quality 1. The UI's `On` state, new default, and legacy PCF migration are all EVSM2 (3).
- "Shadow edges shimmer while the camera dollies" (cascade-blend era) is the RAW render, not the blend: measured with TAA OFF on the `ENGINE_SPLAT_SHADOW_DEBUG=2` attenuation map, parallax-compensated (tile-aligned, so camera motion cannot explain the residual), dolly 0.103 m/frame. Cascade-blend knobs are neutral — transition band `ENGINE_SHADOW_TRANSITION` 0.01/0.1/0.3 = edge 5.761/5.775/5.775 gray, blend dither 0.6 = 5.981 (+4 %), jitter OFF = 5.478. What the metric does move on: TAA ON cuts the same edge residual 5.775 → 2.006 (2.9×, and the dither's cost drops to +6 %), the filter footprint `ENGINE_SHADOW_FILTER_WORLD` 0.04/0.08/0.12 = 5.775/5.144/4.344 (−25 % for 2× penumbra, beauty shadow-edge gradient 8.068 → 8.042 = −0.3 %), and the filterable-vs-hard choice EVSM2 5.775 vs PCF3 12.168 (shadow-area residual 0.753 vs 7.486 = 10×). Fingerprint: the shimmer appears only in `SETTINGS=taaEnabled=false` runs; a pose-matched TAA-on burst measures 0.42 gray over the shadow mask, i.e. invisible. Do not chase it with band/dither knobs — and never A/B it with screen-anchored crops: parallax at the parked vantage is 2.8 px/frame at 40 m, which alone reads as 35 gray at a shadow edge.
- Player-anchored cascade rings apply to every shadow mode, not only PCF: `ShadowMapManager::ConvertToFilterable` consumes `ShadowAttribs` after the engine's ring override, so EVSM gets the same player-relative cascade geometry. Rings off (`ENGINE_SHADOW_RINGS=0`) restores camera-frustum distribution for A/B.
- "Small triangle-shaped shadow, top-right of screen, over terrain, gone with shadows off" = cascade NEAR-plane cross-section: ring cascade z windows are `plz ± 2·radius` (light-z centred on the player), so terrain/props between the sun and the cascade get clipped at the near plane and write depth ≈0 across their projected area — a planar occluder that covers every receiver behind it, with straight box-face edges. Measured signature in the atlas: the map corner holds depth 0.008 (cascade 1, prop write) / 0.298 (cascade 0, cliff write) at the corner texel, and per-draw bisect attributes it to the first caster of that slice. Fix in the ring block: `nearPad = 2·radius·ENGINE_SHADOW_NEAR_PAD` (default 3) dilates the window to `plz − 2r − nearPad … plz + 2r`, putting every caster that can cast into the cascade (cliff base 25 m, cliff wall 850 m, mountains 700 m here) inside the window as a real occluder; the cull follows the caster matrix for free. Cost is depth-only: cascade-0 z quantization 1.2 → 2.3 cm/texel (window 24 → 48 m); PCF slope bias scales with the window (margin 12 → 24 cm), EVSM variance floor scales in world units. A VSM-mode Chebyshev floor of 1e-4 in normalized window units bleeds ≈1 % of window (16 m at cascade 2): VSM needs the floor rescaled per window if mode 2 is ever the shipping mode.
